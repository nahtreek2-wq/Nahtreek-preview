## Cross-screen findings

- Client and Engineer applications now share navy/white/construction-yellow branding. Yellow is limited to interactive emphasis; standard copy remains navy/slate for contrast.
- Loading and empty states are strongest in discovery/requests and weakest in detail/settings/help screens.
- Offline cache is intentionally bounded and does not permit AI generation.
- Android small-screen behavior has evidence from the emulator; large Android and all iOS sizes remain unverified.
- PostgreSQL/Prisma is the current implementation. MongoDB/Mongoose is a requested architectural replacement, not an existing model layer.
